Thicknesses in mm, angles in degrees. Angles are the slope of the top at the bass bar, where the magnets cross the plate. Defaults come from a curtate cycloid cross-arch at the mensur (Playfair, CASJ 2003; Mottola, Savart Journal 2011), measured from the channel to the centre line: violin 58 mm with 11/13/15 mm arch height, viola 68 mm with 12.5/15/17.5, cello 125 mm with 22/26/30, bass 190 mm with 32/38.5/45. The slope at the bar is lower than the average slope from the f-hole to the bar, because the arch flattens toward the centre. Thicknesses are your island estimates: violin 2.5–4 mm, viola 2.8–4.3 mm, cello 3.5–5 mm, bass 6–9 mm. The drawing shapes the top as a half curtate cycloid, x = a·t − b·sin t, y = b − b·cos t with a = l/π and b = h/2 (Mottola 2011), sized for each instrument and arch class and placed so its slope at the bass bar equals the class angle.
