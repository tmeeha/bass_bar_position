Thicknesses in mm, angles in degrees. Angles are the local slope between the f-hole and the bass bar, which is steeper than the average arch slope by about 1.35 on curtate cycloid cross-arches (Playfair, CASJ 2003; Mottola, Savart Journal 2011). Violin: your 11–15 mm underside rise over a 55 mm run. Bass: 32–45 mm outside arch height, assuming about 175 mm of run. Thicknesses are your island estimates: violin 2.5–4 mm, viola 2.8–4.3 mm, cello 3.5–5 mm, bass 6–9 mm. Changes are kept in this browser only.
